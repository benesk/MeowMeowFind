Rules & Controls

  • Objective: Locate exactly one hidden Cat in every row, column, and territory color!
  • Single Tap / Swipe: Instant placement or removal of a ✖ mark. You can drag across multiple tiles to paint marks.
  • Double Tap: Instantly drops down a Cat 🐱. Double tap it again to clear it.
  • Dynamic Hints: Get a helpful logic hint showing you what moves are safe or guaranteed!
