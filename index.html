🐱 MeowMeowFind

❤️❤️❤️

Rules & Gameplay

  • Objective: Locate exactly one hidden Cat in every row, column, and color region!
  • Single Tap & Drag: Quickly paint or erase `✖` marks across rows/columns. It never costs lives!
  • Double Tap: Drop a Cat (🐱, 😻, 😺, 😽) onto a square. Watch out! Placing a cat in the wrong spot costs one ❤️.
  • Note: Standard cats cannot touch adjacent neighbors (even diagonally), but *are* allowed to share long-range diagonal lines.
  • Special Wildcard (10% Chance): In rare games, one full-body Cat (🐈) will appear. **No other cat on the entire board can be on the same row, column, or diagonal line as this specific 🐈.**
  • Revives: If you run out of lives, you can revive to restore them. You get unlimited revives on Easy, up to 2 on Medium, and up to 3 on Hard.
