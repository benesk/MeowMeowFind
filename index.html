Rules & Controls

  • Objective: Find exactly one hidden Cat in every row, column, and color territory!
  • Single Tap/Click: Tap or click any empty grid cell to immediately drop or clear an ✖ mark. Drag smoothly to mass-clear zones.
  • Double Tap/Click: Double-tap or double-click a cell to drop or remove a Cat 🐱.
  • Panning & Zooming: Panning and zooming are disabled on the game board for smooth, responsive gameplay.
  • Recharging Hints: Hints require regular valid moves to charge back up. Easy requires 3 moves, Medium requires 7, and Hard requires 12!
